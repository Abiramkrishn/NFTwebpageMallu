Sacred Heritage Meets Web3

Theyyam Genesis: Living Gods of Malabar

Theyyam is one of humanity’s oldest living ritual dance traditions, originating in the sacred groves (Kavu) of North Malabar, Kerala. Through mesmerizing trance, holy fire embers, and intricate organic face-painting (Mukhathezhuthu), human dancers transcend mortality to embody divine deities and folk heroes. NFTwebpageMallu bridges ancient ritual memory with decentralized Web3 provenance — creating an immutable digital sanctuary where sacred art is celebrated, documented, and directed back to support native ritual artists and craftspeople.

Ecosystem Pillars

Bridging Indigenous Folklore with Decentralized Technology

Sacred Archival

High-fidelity visual curation and digital restoration capturing the rarest Kolams of North Malabar before endangered oral rituals and folk master techniques fade from memory.

Artist Empowerment

Smart contract secondary royalties are directed continuously to traditional ritual performers, percussionists (Chenda), and hereditary costume artisan families across Kerala.

Algorithmic Rarity

Carefully weighted rarity attributes based on authentic ritual characteristics: towering bamboo headgear (Mudi), weapon iconography, ceremonial face motifs, and fire trials.

Decentralized Provenance

Every piece of metadata, high-resolution render, and oral lore recording is pinned across IPFS and Arweave, ensuring permanent decentralized archival without central points of failure.

Patron Privileges

Token holders gain access to physical museum-grade archival prints, exclusive documentary footage of nocturnal temple rituals, and invitations to sacred temple festival gatherings.

Heritage DAO Governance

A democratic cultural collective allowing collectors and cultural researchers to vote on research grants, temple restoration sponsorships, and future regional art collections.
